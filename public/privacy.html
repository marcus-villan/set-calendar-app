Privacy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version: Set stores your plans so you can see them. If you don't sign in, they never leave your device. If you do sign in, they are saved to your account so they appear on your other devices. There are no ads, no analytics, and no trackers, and your data is never sold or shared.

Who runs Set

Set is a personal project by Marcus Villanueva. It is not a company product.

What is stored

  • Your sets: the date, title, emoji, optional time, and optional note that you type.
  • Your settings: your theme and light/dark choice, kept on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and the link to your Google profile picture, which Google shares when you approve the sign-in. Set never sees your Google password and asks for no other Google data (no contacts, no calendar, no files).

Where it is kept

  • Not signed in: only in your browser's storage on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Signed in: also in a database hosted by Supabase (in Singapore). Each account can read and change only its own sets; this is enforced by the database itself.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub can see technical details of requests, such as your IP address. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how they handle that.

What Set does not do

  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No code or fonts loaded from other websites while you use the app.
  • Nobody reads your sets. They are only used to show them back to you.

Your controls

  • Export: Settings → Export backup saves a copy of your sets as a file.
  • Sign out: removes your sets from that device. They stay in your account.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Account → Delete my account and data permanently deletes your account and every set saved to it, right away. This cannot be undone.
  • Sets you delete are kept in your account as hidden "deleted" markers so the deletion reaches your other devices. Deleting your account removes those too.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS, loads nothing from third parties, and blocks injected scripts. Sign-in is handled by Google and Supabase. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't store passwords or other secrets in a set.

Children

Set is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
